# Company-wide cash/accrual reporting

Status: current human-authorized implementation; plan independently approved; implementation under verification. Future features are in ../V2-ROADMAP.md. No posting method or stored history changes.

## User outcome

Company settings offers the existing Cash/Accrual report preference as an effective default. Applicable financial reports honor that company choice, expose a per-report override, and label output/CSV/print with the resolved basis. Existing companies retain their configured value. Selecting cash must change recognition correctly, not relabel accrual figures. Changing the preference never changes another company or historical postings.

## Compatibility

Cash/accrual financial views: P&L, balance sheet, P&L by job/class, income-tax summary, sales by customer/item/rep, expenses by vendor and cash-flow statement. All corresponding totals and dimensions must reconcile on the same basis. Trial balance, general ledger and transaction-detail remain explicitly labelled accrual book-ledger reports; drillthrough from a cash summary must explain that distinction, never claim its ledger rows are the cash projection. Aging/open-item/customer statements, banking/reconciliation, stock quantity/value and statutory sales-tax reports retain their own operational accounting meaning. Company financial report basis does not change sales-tax liability timing.

An omitted basis means Company default for compatible financial reports. Explicit cash/accrual overrides win for that run only. Forms must not submit a hidden/default accrual that defeats the company preference. Unsupported cash selection on a book/operational report must be clear, not silently ignored. Paging/exports/printing preserve basis and restart after relevant preference or settlement changes.

## Recognition contract

Implement one shared, exact, attributable read projection from immutable postings and dated settlement facts. For each cutoff, start with accrual books and defer the unsettled income/expense components of invoice/statement-charge, bill, credit-memo and vendor-credit families against their corresponding AR/AP control accounts. Period figures are the difference between beginning and ending cumulative projections. Use actual retained application/allocation and inverse history, not current paid flags. Currently valid matching supplies attribution; monetary recognition date is the later of source payment effective date and commercial component effective date, not the clerical application date. A January receipt for a January invoice matched in February is January income on rerun; a January prepayment for a February invoice is February income. Unmatching/rematching can restate prior report periods/accounts/dimensions. These are current-books reports, not frozen as-issued snapshots; retain actual posting/reversal dates and never erase a historical cash movement just because its document later changes. Preserve revision, entered-line, item, customer/job/class/vendor attribution and original stock costs.

- Direct sales receipts, checks/card charges, cash expenses and ordinary journals retain posted effects. Bank transfers, deposits of previously received money and card repayments introduce no second income/expense.
- Partly settled documents recognize exact attributable shares; customer allocations already carry net/tax recognition sources. AP shares need a documented deterministic minor-unit allocation across the owning bill's components, preserving signs and residual cents.
- Unapplied receipts/prepayments remain explicit AR/AP balances, consistent with the Desktop management-report convention; allocating them changes attribution, not cash or original payment. Current matching/reversal determines allocation; the money/component dates above determine monetary recognition. Watermarks include all later attribution changes, even beyond the requested cutoff. Do not manufacture a new income account.
- Noncash credit application releases each document's own eligible components at the later of application and both component dates; amounts/accounts/tax/costs need not cancel. Returning an unapplied payment is cash/control only, with no P&L. A commercial-credit refund recognizes that credit's deferred P&L at the later of refund and credit component dates. Applications and refunds share source capacity; exact reversal history prevents double recognition.
- Inventory asset movements and capital/tax/loan balance-sheet components retain their real posted timing. Defer original-cost COGS with the unpaid stock sale; retain offset in AR rather than making inventory quantities or valuation fictional. Stock purchases remain assets/AP until their real changes. Stock returns use retained original cost. Zero-priced sales with positive COGS recognize that original cost on the physical sale date, including later effective-date recost adjustments; no collectible amount exists to wait for. A zero-total sale follows the same physical-cost rule. Zero-cost purchases create quantity/ownership only, not invented expense. Corrections/void/deletion use exact reversal effects.
- Sales-tax liability timing is a separate existing preference. Cash financial reporting must not activate unsupported payment-receipt tax posting or alter historical liability.
- Balance sheet remains balanced; prior/current fiscal earnings tie exactly to same-basis P&L. General journal effects remain posted in both views. No claim of statutory cash-tax compliance.

Reference framing: Intuit's “Differentiate Cash and Accrual basis” describes company summary-report preferences, per-report toggles and journal treatment (https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/report-management/differentiate-cash-accrual-basis/L3RvRm1vW_US_en_US). Its Desktop cash-balance-sheet article documents inventory/assets, unapplied money and tax-related AR/AP residuals (https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-ca/help-article/set-inventory-lists/resolve-ar-ap-balances-cash-basis-balance-sheet/L8XR76bVQ_CA_en_CA). We do not reproduce destructive workaround advice or arbitrary journal-line-order behavior.

## Allocation and reconciliation details

AR revenue uses captured net/tax allocation identities; tax is not income. COGS uses the fraction of the same commercial line's net charge satisfied, separately from tax, applied to its original issue-linked cost and recost. Zero net uses physical-date recognition. AP bill-wide settlements allocate across captured signed purchase components using exact cumulative integer arithmetic and deterministic source order; expense and asset shares together exhaust the gross payable, only P&L shares are deferred. Discounts retain their signed contribution rather than becoming positive expenses; mixed asset/expense bills must not allocate the full payment to expenses. Final settlement exhausts every component exactly. Reject inconsistent source evidence explicitly rather than dropping it.

Cash-flow opening/closing working-capital and income all use the same projected balances so opening cash + income + adjustments equals closing cash. Summary dimensions retain each original component's attribution.
