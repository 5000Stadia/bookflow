Income for the period grouped by the customer or job the sale was made to, in hierarchy-name order so a job reads directly under the customer it belongs to. {% elif summaries.verb == 'sales-by-item' %}

The same income grouped by the item sold, with the quantity in the item's own base unit and the average price that quantity fetched. Average price is income divided by quantity rounded to the cent for reading, and is blank where a line was priced by allocation and carries no quantity. Every sales line names an item, so income with no item is income no sale line posted: an income journal entry, or a deposit taken straight to an income account. That is the row called No item, and the totals above say what it came to, so item income plus no-item income is the income the profit and loss reports for the same dates.

{% elif summaries.verb == 'sales-by-rep' %}

The same income grouped by the sales representative the sale itself captured. The representative is read from the document revision that posted the money, never from the customer's current representative, so reassigning a customer today does not move sales already made. Income from a document that captured no representative is the row called Unassigned.

A line that names no vendor takes the one vendor named elsewhere on the same posting, which is how a cheque's payee reaches its expense lines. A vendor credit is negative. Expense naming no vendor at all is the row called No name. Select a vendor to list that vendor's open bills.
