Indirect method. Net income for the period, then what every other balance-sheet account did to cash: a source of cash is positive and a use of cash is negative, so money put into a receivable or into equipment reads negative and money raised from a lender or an owner reads positive.

Net income plus the three section subtotals plus cash at the start of the period is cash at the end of it, and that closing figure is the total of the bank accounts on the balance sheet for the same date. Difference is that reconciliation; it is zero whenever the books balance, and the statement shows it only when it is not.

Each row is one balance-sheet account's own change; subaccounts appear separately and cash accounts are the subject of the statement rather than rows in it.
