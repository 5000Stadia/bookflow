Restore a backup

Restoring opens a .bookflow-backup file as a new company. Bookflow checks every file in the backup before the company is added, and a damaged backup is refused with nothing added. You become the restored company's owner.

Restoring never replaces a company. If the company in the backup is already here, choose Restore as a copy: the copy gets its own company id and a name of your choosing, and the original is left as it is. A backup from an older Bookflow is brought up to date as it is restored; one from a newer Bookflow is refused until Bookflow is upgraded.
