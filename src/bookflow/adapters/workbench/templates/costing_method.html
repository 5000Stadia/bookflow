Costing method

How stock is costed for every item: average cost (each sale takes the running average) or FIFO (first in, first out: each sale takes the oldest units' cost). A method is meant to be kept; change it only on your accountant's advice. A switch revalues nothing: the stock on hand that day carries on at its value, and from then on cost of goods sold follows the new method.
